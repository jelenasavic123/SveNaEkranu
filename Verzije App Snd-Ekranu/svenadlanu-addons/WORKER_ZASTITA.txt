https://svenadlanu-addon-api.balevasanja.workers.dev/

Worker vec koristi:
- X-Addon-ID header
- JSON: { id, version }
- Origin chrome-extension://<extension-id>
- ALLOWED_EXTENSION_ID
- allowedVersion = 9.0.0

Addon sada koristi isti taj protokol.

Nije menjano:
- content.js
- page_guard.js
- rules_1.json
- rules_2.json
- ikonice
- PWA fajlovi
- postojece funkcije background.js, osim dodate Worker autorizacije pre addDomain() za nove popup domene.
